How a match is ranked

  1. Read the words. Both descriptions are cleaned and compared, so "BLACK purse!!" and "black wallet" still line up on colour and contents.
  2. Count the stops. The distance between the two stations is measured on the Metro network, and an item found before you lost it is ruled out.
  3. Rank the candidates. A trained model combines the clues into one score. A high score is a strong lead, not proof, so staff verify ownership at the desk.
